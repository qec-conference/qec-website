## Venue

QEC 2027 will be held at the [Campus des Cordeliers](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/en/university/rental-conference-facilities/rental-spaces-cordeliers-campus) of Sorbonne Université, 15 Rue de l'École de Médecine, 75006 Paris, in the Latin Quarter on the left bank of the Seine.

## Getting to Paris

Paris is served by two international airports, Charles de Gaulle (CDG) to the north-east and Orly (ORY) to the south. Both are connected to the city centre by public transport; from Charles de Gaulle, the RER B runs directly to Saint-Michel–Notre-Dame, a short walk from the conference venue. Paris is also well connected by high-speed rail to much of western Europe, and we encourage participants to travel by train where that is practical.

## Getting to the venue

The nearest métro station is Odéon (lines 4 and 10), a few minutes' walk from the venue. Cluny–La Sorbonne (line 10) and Saint-Michel (line 4) are also nearby, as is the RER B and C station Saint-Michel–Notre-Dame.
