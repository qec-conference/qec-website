The registration fees are as follows:

|  Fees |  Early Bird  |  Regular  | Onsite |
|:---------|:--------|:--------|:--------|
|Student Registration | $600 | $800 | $1000 |
|General Registration | $700 | $900 | $1100 |

Early-bird registration will be open until April 24th, 2026, AoE.

The registration fees include:

•  All sessions and program materials.

•  Poster session receptions.

•  Banquet Dinner.

•  Daily refreshment breaks and lunches.

•  Optional tutorials on Sunday, June 7th, 2026 (while space is available).

### Code of Conduct
People who register agree to abide by the <a href="/code-of-conduct">conference code of conduct</a>.
